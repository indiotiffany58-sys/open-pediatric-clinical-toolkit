# Clinical Simulation Scenario: Pediatric Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)

## 1. Scenario Overview
* **Target Audience:** Medical students & pediatric residents
* **Simulation Format:** 6-Role Interactive Clinical Dramatization
* **Clinical Focus:** Febrile UTI diagnosis, sample collection methods, anthropometric dosing, and empirical antibiotic selection.

## 2. Role Distribution
1. **Attending Physician / Lead Clinician:** Guides clinical reasoning and oversees treatment plan.
2. **Junior Resident / Medical Student:** Performs history taking, physical examination, and initial weight/height calculations.
3. **Pediatric Triage Nurse:** Reports vital signs, anthropometric percentiles, and administers medication.
4. **Parent / Caregiver:** Provides patient history, symptom timeline, and raises questions regarding treatment.
5. **Laboratory / Diagnostic Specialist:** Reports urinalysis (leukocyte esterase, nitrites, pyuria) and urine culture sensitivity.
6. **Clinical Evaluator / Moderator:** Tracks time, triggers clinical progression, and scores performance against checklist.

## 3. Clinical Checklist & Learning Objectives
- [ ] Accurately plot patient weight and height and calculate BSA.
- [ ] Select appropriate urine collection method based on toilet-training status (catheterization vs. clean-catch).
- [ ] Differentiate between lower tract cystitis and acute pyelonephritis.
- [ ] Calculate exact weight-based empirical antibiotic dose and fluid requirements.
- [ ] Identify criteria for hospital admission vs. outpatient management and renal ultrasound indications.
