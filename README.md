An open-source repository providing structured clinical simulation scenarios, weight-and-height-based pediatric dosing frameworks, and standardized medical board review question banks (MIR / USMLE style) tailored for medical students and educators in resource-limited academic settings.

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Ecosystem Gap
Medical education and clinical simulation resources are predominantly locked behind high-cost proprietary platforms. In Latin America, students and clinical instructors often lack standardized, peer-reviewed, and easily customizable templates for acute care training, pediatric emergency dosing, and high-yield board preparation. 

**`open-pediatric-clinical-toolkit`** bridges this gap by offering freely accessible, structured clinical tools that anyone can fork, adapt, and deploy in university study groups or hospital simulation labs.

## 📦 Core Modules

* **`/simulations`**: Multi-role clinical simulation scripts (e.g., Pediatric UTI management, Status Epilepticus, Shock classification, and Pediatric CPR workflows) with actor prompts, clinical vignettes, and evaluation checklists.
* **`/dosing-calculators`**: Standardized formulas and reference tables for pediatric weight/height dose calculations, fluid resuscitation, and acute care pharmacology.
* **`/question-banks`**: High-yield, multi-option clinical case questions focused on pediatrics and emergency medicine (Iron Deficiency Anemia, Febrile Seizures, Acute Care) with structured rationales.
* **`/community-health`**: Educational templates and preventive medicine outreach materials (e.g., photoprotection and clinical screening guides).
